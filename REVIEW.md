### Examined and Decided NOT a Defect

1. **`compute_late_minutes` grace comparison (`diff > 600`)**: The starter checked `minutes > 10`. Checking `diff_seconds > 600` is strictly more than 10 minutes. A punch at 09:40:00 (exact 10 minutes) yields 0 late minutes; a punch at 09:40:01 (601 seconds) yields 10 late minutes, which strictly conforms to Rule R2.
2. **PyMongo client pooling**: The global `MongoClient` instance created at module level is thread-safe and manages an internal connection pool automatically. This was kept and configured with `tz_aware=True`.
